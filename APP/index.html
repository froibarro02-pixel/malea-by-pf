GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How do I reserve a dress?

Open a dress, select “Reserve this dress,” complete the form, and submit your request. Reservations are checked by the server to help prevent conflicting bookings.

What do the availability labels mean?

Available means the dress is listed as available. Reserved means a reservation may exist, so your requested dates must be checked. Borrowed means the dress is currently out. Unavailable means it cannot currently be reserved.

Does submitting a request guarantee my reservation?

No. Your request must be successfully accepted by the reservation system and confirmed by Maléa by PF.

Can I reserve a dress marked Reserved?

You may submit a request if the form allows it, but the server must verify the selected dates. A conflicting reservation will be rejected.
